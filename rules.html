Code of Conduct

Respect the pack. These rules keep the community strong.

1

Respect Everyone

No harassment, hate speech, toxicity, or personal attacks. Treat every member the way you want to be treated.

2

No Cheating or Exploits

Cheats, hacks, macros that give unfair advantage, or any form of boosting are strictly forbidden. First offense can result in permanent removal.

3

Keep It Clean in Voice & Chat

Excessive toxicity, constant arguing, or disruptive behavior in Discord or in-game will not be tolerated.

4

Follow Discord & Platform Rules

Obey Discord Terms of Service and the rules of any game we play. Breaking platform rules can get the whole community in trouble.

5

Represent the Pack Well

When wearing the Shadow Wolf name or tag, act with integrity. You represent more than yourself.

6

Listen to Leadership

Officers and staff are here to keep things running. Follow reasonable requests and use the proper channels for issues.

7

Have Fun & Improve

We’re here to compete, improve, and enjoy the game together. Bring good energy.

Questions about the rules? Ask in Discord.
